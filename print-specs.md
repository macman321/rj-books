## Stephen's New Friends

## Book Specifications (Amazon KDP)

### Format: Paperback
- **Trim Size:** 8.5" x 8.5" (square picture book — perfect for illustrations)
- **Page Count:** 36 pages (18 spreads)
- **Paper:** White, 60 lb / 90 gsm (crisp color printing)
- **Cover:** Glossy finish (vibrant colors for kids)
- **Binding:** Perfect bound
- **Ink:** Full-color interior

### ISBN
- Amazon KDP provides free ISBN
- Or purchase your own via Bowker (recommended for brand ownership)

### Pricing Strategy
- **Cost:** ~$3.50-4.50 per book (printing)
- **Retail:** $12.99-14.99
- **Royalty:** ~$4-8 per book

---

## Typography

### Interior Text
- **Font:** OpenDyslexic Regular or Comic Neue (kid-friendly, dyslexia-friendly)
- **Size:** 18-20pt
- **Line Height:** 1.5x
- **Alignment:** Left-aligned (ragged right)
- **Color:** Dark charcoal (#333333) on cream/white background
- **Max words per spread:** 30-50 (early reader friendly)

### Title Page
- **Title:** Bold, 48pt, RJ-Books brand colors
- **Subtitle:** Regular, 24pt
- **Author names:** 18pt

## Illustration Placement

### Spreads Layout
- **Full-bleed illustrations:** All spreads edge-to-edge
- **Text placement:** Bottom third or side margin (never over faces/action)
- **Safe zone:** Keep text 0.5" from trim edges
- **Bleed:** 0.125" extra on all sides

### Image Resolution Requirements
- **Minimum:** 300 DPI
- **Recommended:** 350-400 DPI for crisp printing
- **At 8.5" x 8.5":** 2550 x 2550 pixels minimum
- **Current illustrations:** Need to be scaled to print resolution

## Amazon KDP Setup Steps

### 1. Create Account
- Go to kdp.amazon.com
- Sign in with Amazon account
- Complete tax interview (required for royalties)
- Set up payment method (direct deposit)

### 2. Start New Title
- Click "Create" → "Kindle eBook" + "Paperback"
- Enter title: "Stephen's New Friends"
- Subtitle: "A Picture Book About Change and New Friends"
- Series: RJ-Books (Book 1)
- Description: Use blurb below

### 3. Keywords & Categories
**Keywords:**
- children's books about moving
- kids books about new neighbors
- picture books about change
- dog books for kids
- books for kids with anxiety
- early reader friendship stories

**Categories:**
- Children's Books > Growing Up & Facts of Life > Friendship, Social Skills & School Life
- Children's Books > Literature & Fiction > Chapter Books
- Children's Books > Animals > Dogs

### 4. Upload Content
**Interior:**
- Export as PDF (PDF/X-1a:2001 or PDF/X-3:2002)
- Embed all fonts
- Include bleed if using full-bleed images

**Cover:**
- Use KDP Cover Creator or upload custom
- Template size for 8.5" x 8.5", 36 pages: approximately 17.25" x 8.5" (front + back + spine)
- Upload as PDF or high-res image

### 5. Pricing
- **Minimum:** ~$8.50 (cost + Amazon fee)
- **Recommended:** $12.99 - $14.99
- **Expanded distribution:** $16.99 (libraries, bookstores)

### 6. Preview & Publish
- Use KDP Previewer to check formatting
- Order author copy ($3.50-4.50 + shipping) to verify quality
- Hit "Publish" — live in 24-72 hours

## Book Description (Amazon Listing)

**Short (150 words):**
Stephen loves the house across the street—until the day it comes down. With his loyal dog Jackson by his side, Stephen learns that even when things change, something wonderful might be just around the corner.

A gentle picture book about loss, hope, and new friendships. Perfect for children facing changes in their neighborhood, school, or life.

**Long (500 words):**
Stephen is four years old, and he loves his home, his dog Jackson, and the friendly neighbors across the street. But one day, everything changes. The house across the street is torn down, and Stephen is heartbroken.

With the love of his family and the comfort of his faithful German Shepherd Jackson, Stephen learns that change—though scary—can lead to beautiful new beginnings. As he watches a new house being built, excitement slowly replaces sadness. And when a little girl named Nora moves in, Stephen discovers that the best surprises often come from the unexpected.

"Stephen's New Friends" is the first book in the RJ-Books series, created by Ryan Lambiasi. Drawing from personal experiences with ADHD, dyslexia, and life's transitions, Ryan crafts stories that validate children's big feelings while offering hope and gentle guidance.

With dyslexia-friendly text, emotional storytelling, and beautiful watercolor illustrations, this book is perfect for:
- Children ages 3-7
- Kids experiencing change or moving
- Early readers building confidence
- Parents seeking stories about emotional resilience
- Teachers and counselors supporting social-emotional learning
