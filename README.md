# RJ-Books

Stories for big feelings, little hearts.

## About

RJ-Books creates children's picture books that help kids navigate big emotions through gentle storytelling. Founded by childhood friends who understand the challenges of growing up with ADHD, dyslexia, and life's transitions.

## Books

### Stephen's New Friends (Book 1)
A picture book about change, loss, and new friendships.

- **Age:** 3-7 years
- **Pages:** 36
- **Format:** 8.5" x 8.5" paperback
- **Price:** $12.99
- **Status:** Ready for print

**Synopsis:**
Stephen loves the house across the street—until the day it comes down. With his loyal dog Jackson by his side, Stephen learns that even when things change, something wonderful might be just around the corner.
